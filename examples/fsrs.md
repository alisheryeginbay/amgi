# Spaced repetition

Lines that don't start a card are notes and are ignored.

Q: What does **FSRS** stand for?
A: Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler

Q: Which three variables does the FSRS memory model track?
A: Difficulty, Stability and Retrievability
(the DSR model)

C: FSRS schedules a card when its {{retrievability}} drops to the {{desired retention::target}}.
